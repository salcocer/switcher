Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Switcher doesn't collect data about how you use your Mac. Nothing about your windows ever leaves your Mac. The only thing it sends over the internet is what's needed to check your license.

Switcher is a window switcher and window snapper for macOS made by Alejandro Alcocer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Switcher app and this website.

What stays on your Mac

  • Windows. To show the switcher, Switcher reads the titles, positions and apps of your open windows, and, if you allow Screen Recording, takes small pictures of their contents. Thumbnails are kept in memory while Switcher runs. None of this is saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Keyboard. Switcher watches for its own shortcuts (like ⌥Tab and ⌃⌥ with the arrow keys) and the keys you press while the switcher is open. It doesn't record or keep anything you type.
  • Settings are stored in Switcher's preferences on your Mac.
  • Your trial start date and license key are stored in your Mac's Keychain.

What Switcher sends, and to whom

This section is about the version downloaded from this website. The Mac App Store edition sends nothing; see the next section.

License checks: Lemon Squeezy

When you activate a license, and about once a day after that, Switcher sends your license key, an activation ID and your Mac's model identifier (for example “Mac14,2”, never your computer's name) to the license service of Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. Lemon Squeezy answers whether the license is active and with the email address on the subscription, which Switcher shows in Settings → License.

Store settings: GitHub

About once a day, Switcher downloads a small settings file (license.json, with the checkout link and price) from this website, which is hosted by GitHub.

Nothing else

No analytics, crash reports, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share data about you. Like any internet request, the requests above reveal your IP address to Lemon Squeezy and GitHub, who handle it under their own privacy policies. Links in the app (Subscribe, Manage Subscription, this website) open in your web browser.

If you got Switcher from the Mac App Store

The Mac App Store edition doesn't contact Lemon Squeezy or this website, and sends nothing at all. Your subscription is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we only see the sales and subscription reports Apple gives developers, which don't identify you. Everything under “What stays on your Mac” applies the same way, except that this edition keeps no trial date or license key.

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for Switcher: it runs the checkout, takes payment, handles sales tax and sends receipts, under the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy. We receive what we need to provide and support your subscription: your name, email address, billing country and order and subscription details. We never receive your full card details.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages, with downloads from GitHub Releases. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions

  • Accessibility (required) lets Switcher see your windows, catch its shortcuts and move windows when you snap them.
  • Screen Recording (optional) is used only for window thumbnails.

You can turn either off anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Opening at login

Switcher only opens when you log in if you choose that, on first launch or later in Settings → General.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your Mac: to remove everything, first deactivate your license (Settings → License → Deactivate This Mac), then delete Switcher, its preferences file ~/Library/Preferences/com.csaav.Switcher.plist, and the items named com.csaav.Switcher in Keychain Access.
  • Subscription data is kept by Lemon Squeezy and us for as long as needed to provide your subscription and as required for tax and accounting. You can ask us for a copy or to delete it at any time.

Children

Switcher isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or open an issue at github.com/salcocer/switcher/issues. Issues are public, so please don't post personal details there.
